# IMAGE GENERATION LOG

Record of generated conceptual assets integrated into the portfolio. No private
source material, credentials, customer data, real logos, or production telemetry
was supplied to the image model.

## Generation and optimisation notes

- Generator: OpenAI image generation.
- Source method: every asset was generated as a standalone full-frame image; no project image was cropped from a contact sheet.
- Quality: native generated resolution was preserved. Images were never enlarged beyond their source dimensions.
- Optimisation: exported as metadata-free WebP at quality 92 using high-effort encoding.
- Social preview: downscaled and centre-cropped to 1200×630 from a larger standalone source.
- Labelling: all generated project artwork is described as a conceptual visualization in the interface and alt text.
- Grid texture was deliberately removed at the owner's request.

## Factual boundary

The generated images are conceptual artwork. They are not documentary product
photos, real customer environments, training frames, captured telemetry, or
production screenshots. The violence-detection metrics and engineering details
in the case study are owner-supplied measured results; the accompanying CCTV
artwork remains conceptual.
